02. Problem Statement

Critical Gaps in Traditional Allocation Systems

Why legacy manual spreadsheets and paper forms fail students and administrators.

✕

Gap 1: Opaque Discretion & Clerical Favoritism

Spreadsheet allocations take weeks, contain frequent clerical typos, and lack transparency, leading to student distrust and accusations of favoritism.

✕

Gap 2: Discordant Roommate Pairings

Random room assignments pair students with opposite diurnal rhythms (early bird vs. night owl) or conflicting study noise needs, sparking roommate hostility and distress.

✕

Gap 3: Disability & Accessibility Breaches

Physical disability requirements are overlooked, assigning mobility-impaired students to upper floors without elevator access due to lack of hard programmatic checks.

✕

Gap 4: Sensitive Lifestyle Privacy Leaks

Students' personal lifestyle habits and medical accommodations sit unencrypted in open sheets and physical forms, breaching basic data privacy standards.

✕

Gap 5: Zero Auditability & Inability to Explain Decisions

Colleges cannot explain why Student A received their top choice while Student B was waitlisted. Overrides happen off-the-record without an immutable log.

05. Algorithm Architecture

Deterministic Multi-Pass Heuristic Allocation Engine

Pure TypeScript library (zero I/O, zero external framework imports) designed for speed and explainability.

A

PASS A • Quota & PWD Accessibility Hard Priority

Strictly maps mobility-impaired (PWD) applicants to ground-floor wheelchair-accessible beds (Invariant I8). Allocates categorical reserved quotas (SC/ST/OBC, Sports, Scholarship, and International exchange students) before general merit processing.

B

PASS B • General Merit & Ranked Preference Greedy Match

Sorts general applicants by composite score (academic standing CGPA + geographic distance index). Iterates greedily through applicant ranked choices (hostels 1..N and room types). Resolves ties deterministically using seeded SFC32 PRNG.

C

PASS C • Waitlist Buffering & Fallback Resolution

Handles over-subscribed hostels deterministically without exceeding structural capacity. Assigns unplaced students to fallback campus residences or deterministic waitlist queues while protecting buffer margins for emergency reallocations.

Seed Generator: cyrb128(seedString) • PRNG: sfc32 (Simple Fast Counter) • 0 Math.random() calls
06. Roommate Matching Model

Lifestyle Compatibility Heuristic & Privacy Guarantees

4-dimensional normalized vector matching with cryptographic AES-256-GCM data shielding.

4-Dimensional Evaluation Vector

1. Sleep Schedule (Weight: 35%) Aligns Early Birds with Early Birds, Night Owls with Night Owls. Eliminates nocturnal light/alarm disturbance.
2. Study Habits & Noise (Weight: 25%) Distinguishes students who need dead silence in-room from those who study with background music or study in libraries.
3. Cleanliness & Organization (Weight: 20%) Matches neat & tidy students together to avoid shared space clutter disputes.
4. Social Tolerance & Guests (Weight: 20%) Scores tolerance for room visitors, gaming, and musical instrument practice.

Mathematical Model & Cryptography

10. Invariants & Security

8 Non-Negotiable System Invariants (I1–I8)

Architectural and mathematical integrity guarantees enforced across code and database layers.

I1 & I2 • Draft/Published Separation & Immutability Strict state machine (`DRAFT` → `UNDER_REVIEW` → `APPROVED` → `PUBLISHED`). Exactly one atomic code path publishes drafts in a transaction. Published records are permanently immutable.
I3 • Deterministic Reproducibility Identical applicant snapshot + seed + rules + engine version guarantees byte-identical draft output. Verified via 200+ random property runs.
I4 • Full Structured Explainability Every bed assignment stores structured metadata: preference rank, verified constraints, quota bucket, PRNG tiebreak, compatibility score, and rationale.
I5 • Mandatory Override Justification All administrative reassignments require categorized justification (≥10 chars). Hard constraint exceptions mandate Chief Warden escalation.
I6 • Sensitive Lifestyle Privacy Questionnaires encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Never returned to staff; pairwise compatibility scores only. Deletable upon request with tombstone audit.
I7 & I8 • Hash-Chained Audit & Hard Constraints Append-only SHA-256 hash-chained audit log with tamper detection. Hard constraints (capacity, gender, PWD accessibility) are never violated.
